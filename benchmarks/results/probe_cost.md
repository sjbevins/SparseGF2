Every time is **one thread** (except in the all-threads section), after a warm-up; single-thread times are inflated directly by any background load. During the run the machine was otherwise idle except for background services, including a local LLM server (about 0.4 of one core when sampled before the run); background activity inflates single-thread times, so treat differences under about 10 % as noise.

Generated by `python benchmarks/probe_cost.py`; total 5.8 min. Estimates are `api._trajectory_us` of sparsegf2 3.0.0.dev12.

States (one trajectory, probes at the final layer): **single**, reference `single` after 2 n ceil(log2 n) random all-to-all gates (k = 1, erasures fail at about n / 2 qubits: the worst case of the erasure scans); **purification**, the same scramble with every system qubit paired (k = n, a volume-law state on 2n qubits: the worst case of the entanglement eliminations); **monitored**, the purification state after min(n / 2, 256) further layers on a Watts-Strogatz graph (k = 4, beta = 0.1, `random_edges`) at measurement rate 0.05. A measurement is the trajectory with R copies of the probe at its final layer minus the trajectory without probes, divided by R; medians over repeats. Where the copies take under 5 % of the trajectory without probes the row is timing noise, marked unresolved and left out of the smallest ratios. Rows with estimate / measured below 1.5 are marked **!**.
